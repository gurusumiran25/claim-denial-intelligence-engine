Claim 9018: CO-16 (Claim lacks required information)

Recommendation: Submit a corrected claim (do not file an appeal).
Why: The claim was missing information needed to process it.

Next steps
- Do not file an appeal: Medicare treats a claim with missing or invalid information as unprocessable, with no appeal rights. [1]
- Correct the missing fields and submit a new claim; appealing only delays payment and risks a timely-filing denial. [2]
