Claim 9003: CO-29 (Timely filing)

Recommendation: Do not appeal yet.
Why: The claim was filed after the general 12-month Medicare limit and no exception has been documented.

Next steps
- Check whether a filing-limit exception applies (retroactive entitlement, administrative error) and gather the documentation. [1]
- A timely-filing denial is usually handled by an exception request, not a standard appeal. [1]

Staff checklist
- Confirm the payer's own filing limit in the contract; if it is shorter than 12 months, replace the general Medicare limit in the letter.
- Attach proof of the original submission date.
- Confirm the payer's own rules; replace general Medicare statements where they differ.
